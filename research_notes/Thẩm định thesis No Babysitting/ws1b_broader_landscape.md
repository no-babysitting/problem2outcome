# WS1b — Broader Competitive Landscape for the "No Babysitting" (NB) Thesis (beyond the 8 coding-agent plugins)

Scope note: covers AI coaching / AI literacy / enterprise AI enablement, problem framing and spec-driven development (SDD), non-coding agentic workflow tools, prompt optimization, evaluation/observability (brief), personal assistants with memory (brief), and HCI research prototypes. The 8 known plugins (ECC, Hermes Agent, Hallmark, REA, Superpowers, Compound Engineering, GSD, BMAD) are excluded on purpose because another researcher covers them. Research date: 2026-11-17. Tags: [Fact] = stated by a primary or credible source; [Finding] = pattern across sources; [Inference] = my reasoning; [Hypothesis] = needs testing. "Shipped" means generally available; "announced" means roadmap or preview.

Method and limits: about 30 web searches and fetches, plus shallow clones of `github/spec-kit` and `Fission-AI/OpenSpec` to check commit cadence. Many funding and traction figures come from company press releases or secondary aggregators, and they are marked that way. No user interviews were done. Reddit and HN sentiment was sampled only lightly.

---

## Q1. What relevant products, projects and methodologies exist, and how do they compare on the NB dimensions?

### Takeaway
I found 28 relevant items. None of them combines all five NB elements: framing, adaptive coaching, workflow design, evaluating both outcome and collaboration, and learning over time, while delegating execution to native platforms. The pieces exist separately, though. Vendors ship collaboration frameworks and coaching agents: Anthropic AI Fluency (4D + Fluency Index) and Microsoft Prompt Coach. Enterprise upskilling players are moving into "AI coaching": Multiverse Atlas, Section, Sana/Workday. Measurement startups now score "AI fluency": Larridin, Workhelix. SDD tools own problem-to-spec for developers: Spec Kit, Kiro, OpenSpec, Tessl. ChatPRD owns it for PMs. The closest analogues are enterprise-sold, and they either teach (courses, coaches) or measure (telemetry). None coaches inside a specific real task and then evaluates that collaboration.

### Cited Findings

**A. AI fluency / AI literacy / enterprise enablement (most relevant category)**

1. **Anthropic AI Fluency (4D Framework, courses, AI Fluency Index)**. Classification: Conceptual reference, plus the biggest native threat. *Deep dive 1.*
   - [Fact] The framework has four competencies: Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence. It was built with professors Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller. The free course "AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations" runs about 3 hours on Coursera and Anthropic Academy. Spin-offs exist for builders, small businesses, nonprofits (with GivingTuesday) and educators (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Sources: [Anthropic Academy – AI Fluency for builders](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-for-builders), [Coursera course page](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-fluency-framework-foundations/reviews), [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/anthropic-ai-fluency-course/). Lesson counts differ between sources: one says 12 lessons, a university guide says 2 modules of 10 ([Pitt KB](https://services.pitt.edu/TDClient/33/Portal/KB/ArticleDet?ID=3341)).
   - [Fact] The **AI Fluency Index** was published in February 2026. It analyzed 9,830 multi-turn Claude.ai conversations from one week in January 2026. The framework defines 24 behaviors, but only 11 are observable in chat, so the study measured those 11. Conversations with iteration and refinement showed about 2.67 additional fluency behaviors, against 1.33 without. In the roughly 12.3% of conversations that produced artifacts, description and delegation behaviors rose, while discernment behaviors fell: identifying missing context, fact-checking, and questioning reasoning. Anthropic notes the sample likely skews toward early adopters. Sources: [Anthropic research – AI Fluency Index](https://anthropic.com/research/AI-fluency-index), [The Decoder, Feb 2026](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-ai-fluency-index-finds-that-polished-ai-output-makes-users-less-likely-to-check-for-errors/), [Gigazine, 2026-02-24](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260224-ai-fluency-index). Secondary coverage varies slightly on figures, so check the primary page before quoting them.
   - Assessment. Purpose: teach and measure human-AI collaboration competencies. Users: everyone, segmented by audience. Interaction model: courses plus aggregate research, not in-task coaching. Adapts to the user: no. Evaluates collaboration: yes, at population level from chat logs. Platform: Claude-centric but framework-agnostic. Onboarding friction: low, free. Maturity: shipped, backed by Anthropic.

2. **Microsoft 365 Copilot "Prompt Coach" agent**. Classification: Adjacent / native threat (S2/S3 in enterprise). *Deep dive 2.*
   - [Fact] It is a prebuilt Microsoft agent that helps users rewrite unclear or underperforming prompts. It does not run them; users take the refined prompt to other agents such as Researcher or Analyst. It is also available as an agent-builder template and as an open-source PnP declarative-agent sample. Sources: [Microsoft Learn – Examine the Prompt Coach agent](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-in/training/modules/explore-prebuilt-microsoft-365-copilot-agents/7-examine-prompt-coach-agent), [Microsoft Learn – agent template](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/agent-template-prompt-coach), [PnP sample](https://pnp.github.io/copilot-pro-dev-samples/samples/da-PromptCoach/), [Visual Studio Magazine, 2026-01-22](https://visualstudiomagazine.com/articles/2026/01/22/how-to-use-the-prompt-coach-agent-to-create-effective-prompts-for-copilot.aspx?p=1).
   - [Finding] Its scope is prompt-level, not problem-level. It has no outcome or collaboration evaluation and no documented cross-session adaptation. It still shows that the largest enterprise AI vendor ships a "coach" for non-technical users at no extra cost ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-in/training/modules/explore-prebuilt-microsoft-365-copilot-agents/7-examine-prompt-coach-agent)).

3. **Native "learning modes": ChatGPT Study Mode, Claude Learning mode, Gemini Guided Learning**. Classification: Adjacent / native.
   - [Fact] ChatGPT Study Mode launched in July 2025, Gemini Guided Learning in August 2025, and Claude's learning mode in April 2025 for Education, later expanded to all users. All three use Socratic or guided questioning instead of direct answers. Claude's mode works inside Projects. Sources: [Pearson blog, Feb 2026](https://pearson.com/international-schools/international-schools-blog/2026/02/what-is-gemini_s-guided-learning-and-chatgpts-study-mode-.html), [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/claudes-new-learning-modes-take-on-chatgpts-study-mode-heres-what-they-do), [EdTech Insiders](https://edtechinsiders.substack.com/p/study-modes-the-new-ai-battleground). Mostly secondary sources; dates not confirmed on vendor pages.
   - [Inference] Platforms can switch Socratic, "don't just answer" behavior on with a toggle. These modes are aimed at learning subject content, not at framing and delivering work outcomes. Still, the interaction mechanics NB would need are already native.

4. **Multiverse (Atlas AI coaching platform)**. Classification: Adjacent, the closest enterprise analogue for S2/S3. *Deep dive 3.*
   - [Fact] Multiverse raised $70M at a $2.1B valuation in May 2026, led by Schroders Capital, for European expansion and the rollout of the Atlas AI-coaching platform. It acquired StackFuel in January 2026. It reports its first cash-positive quarter (Jan–Mar 2026), which is company-reported. Sources: [Multiverse blog](https://www.multiverse.io/blog/multiverse-raises-70-million-europes-ai-adoption-platform), [Dealroom, 2026-05-15](https://dealroom.co/news/128958-multiverse-raises-70m-at-2-1b-valuation-to-build-europes-ai-adoption-lay/), [IT Brief UK](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/multiverse-raises-usd-70m-to-expand-ai-training-in-europe).
   - [Fact, secondary] Atlas reportedly tripled daily active users over the year. Multiverse has pivoted toward AI training for functions beyond junior developer roles. Named clients include Microsoft, Mars and John Lewis. Source: [Enterprise DNA](https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/multiverse-70m-ai-upskilling-workforce-2026). Not independently verified.
   - Gap: I could not confirm from primary docs how Atlas coaches (in-task or curriculum), whether it adapts, or whether it evaluates collaboration. Total funding of $570M is reported by [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/multiverse-raises-570m-ai-training-valuation/), which is a weak source.

5. **Section (Section AI / formerly Section School)**. Classification: Adjacent / conceptual reference (S2 managers and knowledge workers).
   - [Fact] Section publishes a twice-yearly AI Proficiency Report and tracks employees across novice, experimenter, practitioner and expert levels. The June 2025 report found proficiency "flatlining" and AI knowledge scores going down. CEO Greg Shove: "when 25% of the workforce doesn't know what to use AI for, companies will not get ROI." Sources: [Section – AI Proficiency Report](https://www.sectionai.com/ai/ai-proficiency-report), [Section blog, 2025-06-13](https://www.sectionai.com/blog/new-data-on-ai-proficiency), [BusinessWire, 2025-06-10](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250610142741/en).
   - [Fact] A Section post argues that "being good at prompting isn't enough" and points to workflow redesign ([Section blog](https://www.sectionai.com/blog/being-good-at-prompting-isnt-enough)). Section offers a free tool called "ProfAI" ([Section blog](https://www.sectionai.com/blog/free-ai-training)), but I could not verify what it does.
   - [Inference] Section's public message is "prompting isn't enough; redesign workflows." It is the closest *conceptual* match to NB's thesis among enablement vendors, which means the narrative is already in the market.

6. **Larridin**. Classification: Adjacent / technical-enabler-like / threat (enterprise measurement). *Deep dive 4.*
   - [Fact] Larridin raised a $30M Series A on 2026-11-02, led by Pelion Ventures. a16z and Gradient are existing investors, and total funding is $47M. Its modules are AI Adoption, **AI Fluency**, AI Impact, Workflow Intelligence, AI Dev Productivity, and AI Token Spend. Customers include SurveyMonkey, Vertiv, Klaviyo and Gainsight. The press release does not say what "AI Fluency" measures and says nothing about coaching or nudges. Source: [Larridin press release](https://larridin.com/press-releases/larridin-raises-30-million-in-series-a-to-prove-ais-impact-across-the-human-and-agent-workforce).
   - [Inference] Enterprise buyers are paying to *measure* AI fluency at org level. That is the B2B "evaluation of collaboration" niche, approached through telemetry rather than coaching. Larridin could add per-employee nudges and coaching on top of its data.

7. **Workhelix**. Classification: Adjacent (enterprise, services-led).
   - [Fact] $15M Series A led by AIX Ventures, with Andrew Ng's AI Fund, Accenture Ventures and Bloomberg Beta. It breaks jobs into tasks and scores each task's suitability for AI. Sources: [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/workhelix-15-million-series-a-raised-for-maximizing-value-of-ai-investments), [Enterprise AI World](https://www.enterpriseaiworld.com/Articles/News/News/Workhelix-Secures-%2415M-to-Assist-Organizations-Turn-AI-Hype-into-Business-Value-168281.aspx). Round date unclear; one source places it in early 2025.
   - [Inference] Task-level "where should AI apply" analysis is the org-level version of NB's "Capability Blindness" gap.

8. **Workday + Sana**. Classification: Adjacent (enterprise L&D plus agents).
   - [Fact] Workday agreed on 2025-09-16 to acquire Sana for about $1.1B, and the deal has since closed. Sana Learn combines an LMS, course generation and personalized tutoring via learning agents. Sana Agents automates tasks across enterprise systems. Sources: [Workday newsroom](https://newsroom.workday.com/2025-09-16-Workday-Signs-Definitive-Agreement-to-Acquire-Sana), [Josh Bersin](https://joshbersin.com/2025/09/workday-acquires-sana-to-transform-its-learning-platform-and-much-more/), [IT Brief UK](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/workday-completes-sana-deal-enhancing-ai-driven-workplace-tools). Bersin also lists Uplimit, Arist and Docebo as AI-native L&D vendors in the same space.

9. **General AI coaching platforms: BetterUp, CoachHub, Culture Amp AI Coach, Flourish**. Classification: Adjacent (career and performance coaching, not AI-collaboration coaching).
   - [Fact, secondary] BetterUp released a pure AI coaching app in August 2025 after acquiring Heyday and Practica. Culture Amp launched "AI Coach" in 2025. CoachHub offers AI-driven enterprise coaching. Flourish reportedly raised a $500M Series A in June 2026 for coaching and peer mental-health support (not verified against a primary source). Sources: [Wikipedia – BetterUp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BetterUp), [Wikipedia – Culture Amp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_Amp), [Failory list](https://www.failory.com/startups/training), [AlleyWatch, Jul 2026](https://www.alleywatch.com/2026/07/global-startup-funding-top-largest-june-2026-vc/).
   - [Inference] These vendors have the coaching UX, enterprise distribution and HR budget lines. "Coach employees on how they work with AI" is a natural extension for them.

10. **Commodity AI upskilling (DataCamp, Coursera, Pluralsight, General Assembly, etc.)**. Classification: Adjacent / commoditized.
   - [Fact, secondary] One cited DataCamp study says 82% of enterprise leaders report offering some AI training, yet 59% still report an AI skills gap and only 35% have a mature program. A Disco guide cites Deloitte's 2026 State of AI in the Enterprise report calling the skills gap the biggest barrier to AI integration. Sources: [DataCamp blog](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/best-online-platforms-for-enterprise-upskilling-in-ai), [Disco](https://www.disco.co/blog/enterprise-ai-upskilling). These are vendor-relayed figures; I did not verify the primary studies.

**B. Problem framing / requirements elicitation / spec-driven development**

11. **GitHub Spec Kit**. Classification: Adjacent (S1, edging into S2 tech leads).
   - [Fact] It is a CLI plus slash commands. The workflow is Constitution → Specify → Plan → Tasks, and it works with many coding agents ([Böckeler on martinfowler.com, 2025-10-15](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/sdd-3-tools.html)). Activity: in a shallow clone (2026-11-17), the most recent 200 commits span only 2026-10-21 to 2026-11-17, so cadence is very high. Latest commit `5de86dc` is dated 2026-11-17. The repo has `extensions/`, `integrations/` and `bundles/` directories ([repo](https://github.com/github/spec-kit), checked locally).
   - [Fact] Böckeler's critiques: overkill for small features, verbose markdown that is tedious to review, agents that ignore specs (a "false sense of control"), and no clear answer on who writes specs (developers, product people, or both) ([martinfowler.com](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/sdd-3-tools.html)).

12. **Kiro (AWS)**. Classification: Adjacent / threat (S1).
   - [Fact] It is a VS Code-based agentic IDE. Spec mode follows Requirements (EARS-style GIVEN/WHEN/THEN user stories) → Design → Tasks, with "steering" files acting as memory ([martinfowler.com](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/sdd-3-tools.html)). It was announced on 2025-07-14 and hit usage caps during preview because of demand ([The Register, 2025-07-21](https://www.theregister.com/2025/07/21/aws_kiro_usage_cap/)). The GA date conflicts: November 2025 per some sources, 2026-05-07 per [ToolDirectory](https://tooldirectory.ai/tools/kiro) and the [AWS Builder Center](https://builder.aws.com/content/3JtHYrCxV6ZQhhZmDdMvfTd9xEo/amazon-aws-kiro-the-spec-driven-agentic-ide-reshaping-cloud-development). Pricing is credit-based and freemium, with Pro reported at about $20/month (third-party figure).
   - [Fact] Böckeler: Kiro turned a small bug into "4 user stories with 16 acceptance criteria", which is a problem of fitting the process to the task ([martinfowler.com](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/sdd-3-tools.html)).

13. **OpenSpec (Fission-AI)**. Classification: Adjacent (S1).
   - [Fact] Lightweight SDD for coding agents. Its latest tag is `v2.1.0`, and the shallow clone shows 97 commits since 2026-10-01, with the latest on 2026-11-17 ([repo](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec), checked locally). Japanese comparisons also list cc-sdd and Zenflow as SDD tools ([issoh.co.jp](https://www.issoh.co.jp/tech/details/8732/)).

14. **Tessl**. Classification: Technical enabler / Adjacent (it pivoted).
   - [Fact] Tessl started as spec-as-source SDD (Tessl Framework, private beta in 2025). It then paused the Framework to focus on a **registry of evaluated agent skills**, positioning itself as an "Agent Enablement Platform." Skills launched on 2026-01-29 with review evals for more than 2,000 skills. Sources: [Tessl blog, 2026-01-29](https://tessl.io/blog/skills-are-software-and-they-need-a-lifecycle-introducing-skills-on-tessl/), [issoh.co.jp](https://www.issoh.co.jp/tech/details/11690/), [Ry Walker research](https://rywalker.com/research/tessl). Funding of about $125M is reported only by aggregators and is unverified.
   - [Inference] A well-funded SDD pioneer moving away from spec-as-source toward skill distribution plus evals is a signal. Heavyweight methodology was harder to sell than packaged, evaluated capabilities.

15. **ChatPRD**. Classification: Adjacent, the closest S2 (PM) problem-to-spec product. *Deep dive 5.*
   - [Fact, company-reported] An AI copilot for PMs to draft and refine PRDs, one-pagers and plans. It claims 100,000+ PMs and 750,000+ documents as of June 2026, earlier reported "six figures" revenue in its first 6 months with 20–30% month-on-month organic growth, and offers Pro at about $180/year. Founder Claire Vo was CPO of LaunchDarkly and later went full-time on ChatPRD. Sources: [Ry Walker research](https://rywalker.com/research/chatprd), [ChatPRD – first year](https://chatprd.ai/blog/chatprd-the-first-year), [ChatPRD – Claire full time](https://www.chatprd.ai/blog/claire-full-time-on-chatprd), [Every podcast](https://every.to/podcast/best-of-the-pod-she-built-an-ai-product-manager-bringing-in-six-figures-as-a-side-hustle). No audited figures.
   - [Inference] S2 users pay for *artifact-centric, role-specific* framing help ("give me a better PRD"), not for generic collaboration coaching. That supports the counter-thesis "a simpler task-specific solution beats a general methodology."

16. **Founder and product idea-framing tools (Ainna, Fonda, AICofounder/Buildpad, CoFounder.AI)**. Classification: Adjacent (weak evidence).
   - [Fact, marketing] Ainna (Dublin, founded 2025) frames and validates product ideas and grades concepts on 10 dimensions ([ainna.ai](https://ainna.ai/)). AICofounder claims about 70k users with persistent project memory, a self-reported figure ([bigideasdb](https://bigideasdb.com/best-ai-cofounder-platforms-2026)). No independent traction data.

**C. Agentic workflow / context engineering for non-coding work**

17. **Claude Cowork + plugins**. Classification: Integration partner and native threat (S2/S3).
   - [Fact] Cowork launched on 2026-01-12 as a desktop agent that brings Claude Code capabilities to non-technical users through a folder-based interface. Plugins arrived on 2026-01-30 and bundle skills, connectors, commands and sub-agents per job function. Anthropic open-sourced 11 plugins, available on Pro and Max. Sources: [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/anthropic-brings-plugins-to-cowork/), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-cowork-gets-plugins-that-turn-claude-into-a-specialized-assistant-for-knowledge-workers/?cmpscreencustom=1), [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-says-new-cowork-plugins-tailor-claude-specific-job-functions). (WS2 covers native details.)

18. **OpenAI Agent Builder (AgentKit) and OpenAI Evals**. Classification: native (deprecated), a platform-churn signal.
   - [Fact] OpenAI's deprecations page lists **Agent Builder as deprecated (notice 2026-06-03, shutdown 2026-11-30), with "Agents SDK or ChatGPT Workspace Agents" as the replacement.** It lists the **Evals dashboard, API and graders as read-only, shutting down 2026-11-30, with Promptfoo as the replacement.** The prompt optimizer has no entry. Source: [OpenAI API deprecations](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations) (fetched 2026-11-17).
   - [Inference] Native no-code agent builders and native eval tooling can be withdrawn within about 14 months of launch (Agent Builder launched at DevDay in October 2025). NB should not depend on any single platform's builder surface. The same churn also shows that platform vendors are consolidating on agents in the chat product (Workspace Agents), which pushes workflow design toward native chat.

19. **Zapier Agents, n8n, Lindy, Make, Gumloop (workflow automation)**. Classification: Integration partner / Adjacent.
   - [Finding, secondary] Zapier is generally rated easiest for non-technical users (8,000+ integrations, MCP endpoints). n8n suits teams that want control and self-hosting but is more technical ([CodeConductor](https://codeconductor.ai/blog/n8n-vs-zapier-vs-openai-agent-builder-comparison/), [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/agentkit-vs-zapier)). These tools execute workflows; they do not frame problems or coach.

**D. Prompt optimization (largely commoditized)**

20. **Anthropic Console prompt improver / OpenAI prompt optimizer / third-party prompt enhancers**. Classification: Commoditized / Technical enabler.
   - [Fact] Anthropic shipped a prompt improver in its developer console in November 2024 (chain-of-thought rewriting; +30% accuracy on one internal classification test). OpenAI's optimizer is in the API dashboard. Both are aimed at developers and tied to their own platform. Many free third-party "prompt improvers" exist. Sources: [Claude blog – prompt improver](https://claude.com/blog/prompt-improver), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-adds-claude-powered-prompt-optimizer-to-its-ai-development-console/), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/291870/promptly-decrypt-ai-prompt-improver/), [BenchLM](https://benchlm.ai/tools/prompt-optimizer). Microsoft Prompt Coach (item 2) is the end-user equivalent.

**E. Evaluation & observability (enablers, brief)**

21. **Langfuse (ClickHouse), Braintrust, Promptfoo, Helicone (Mintlify)**. Classification: Technical enablers (developer-facing).
   - [Fact] ClickHouse acquired Langfuse on 2026-01-16 alongside a $400M Series D. Langfuse stays open-source and self-hostable and covers traces, prompt management, evals and experiments ([ClickHouse blog](https://clickhouse.com/blog/clickhouse-acquires-langfuse-open-source-llm-observability), [BusinessWire, 2026-01-16](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20260116008883/en/ClickHouse-Raises-$400M-Series-D-Led-by-Dragoneer-to-Accelerate-Expansion-Across-Analytics-and-AI-Infrastructure)). Helicone went to Mintlify on 2026-03-03 ([HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/two-acquisitions-deep-two-still-standing-whats-really-happening-to-open-source-llm-observability)). OpenAI names Promptfoo as its Evals replacement ([OpenAI deprecations](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations)).
   - [Inference] These tools evaluate *model or app output* for builders. None evaluates a *human's* collaboration quality. They could serve as NB back-end enablers (LLM-as-judge harnesses) but are overkill for an MVP.

**F. Personal AI assistants with memory/adaptation (brief, mostly demand signals)**

22. **Dot (New Computer)**: shut down. [Fact] Dot, a personalized AI companion, announced its shutdown in September 2025 and ran until 2025-10-05. Reported causes conflict: a founder "ideological rift" versus safety and competitive pressure ([TechCrunch, 2025-09-05](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/05/personalized-ai-companion-app-dot-is-shutting-down), [CO/AI](https://getcoai.com/news/ai-companion-app-dot-shuts-down-amid-founder-disputes/)).
23. **Pi (Inflection)**: pivoted. [Fact] In March 2024 the co-founders and most of the staff joined Microsoft. Inflection pivoted to enterprise custom models, and Pi, a coach-like supportive assistant, largely stalled ([Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.in/tech/news/inflections-implosion-and-chatgpts-stall-reveal-ais-consumer-problem/articleshow/108648459.cms), [Section blog](https://www.sectionai.com/blog/what-happened-to-inflection-and-pi)).
24. **Limitless (formerly Rewind)**: acquired by Meta in December 2025. Hardware sales stopped and service ended in the EU/UK ([Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/meta-acquires-ai-startup-limitless-ending-sales-of-its-popular-pendant-wearable), [UC Today](https://uctoday.com/meta-acquires-limitless-ai-wearables)).
   - [Inference] Standalone consumer "AI that knows and coaches you" products have struggled or been absorbed. Platform-native memory (ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini, covered in WS2/WS5) is winning, which supports B6 ("prefer native memory").

**G. Human-AI interaction research prototypes / conceptual references**

25. **Tankelevitch et al., "The Metacognitive Demands and Opportunities of Generative AI" (CHI 2024)**. Conceptual reference. [Fact] It argues that GenAI imposes metacognitive demands (self-awareness of goals, task decomposition, evaluating output) and proposes planning aids, reflective prompts and feedback mechanisms as support ([paper PDF](https://advait.org/files/tankelevitch_2024_GenAI_metacognition.pdf), [arXiv 2312.10893](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2312.10893)). This is the closest academic basis for NB's "Interaction / Problem-framing / Evaluation gap" framing.
26. **MetaCLASS (arXiv 2602.02457, February 2026)**. Conceptual reference, and a direct warning. [Fact] In this benchmark of LLMs as metacognitive coaches, the best model predicted the correct coaching move only 43.2% of the time. Models showed a "compulsive intervention bias": when silence was the correct move, they chose it about 4% of the time ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.02457v1)). This is a benchmark, not a user study.
27. **Bloom LLM health coach (Stanford, CHI 2026 Best Paper)**. Conceptual reference. [Fact] Users with the LLM coach increased physical activity by about the same amount as users of the app without LLM features, but their mindsets improved. The team also reported that prompting alone did not work at first ([Stanford News, Apr 2026](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/04/ai-health-coach-mindset)).
28. **LLM goal-setting chatbots (CHI 2026)**. Conceptual reference. [Fact] The study compares chatbot variants that guide users through goal-setting theory and implementation intentions ([arXiv 2602.08636](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08636v1)). Detailed results not extracted.

## Q2. Competitive density per segment (S1 / S2 / S3). Is emptiness in S3 a gap or a signal of weak demand?

### Takeaway
S1 is saturated: SDD tools, plugins, coding agents with native planning modes and evals, all actively maintained. S2 is moderately crowded, but with *role-specific artifact tools* (ChatPRD for PMs) and *enterprise enablement* (Section, Prompt Coach, Cowork plugins), not with "problem-to-outcome coaches." S3 is not empty. It is served top-down by enterprise L&D, AI-coaching platforms and native coaching agents (Prompt Coach, learning modes), with buyers paying for training and measurement. What is nearly empty is *self-serve, individual, in-task coaching for S3*, and the evidence leans toward weak individual demand: consumer coach/companion products shut down or pivoted, and users say they "want results, not prompting lessons." Enterprise demand for S3 enablement, by contrast, is real and funded.

### Cited Findings
- **S1 density: very high.** Spec Kit's last 200 commits fit in under 4 weeks (2026-10-21 to 2026-11-17), and OpenSpec shows 97 commits since 2026-10-01 (local clone of [github/spec-kit](https://github.com/github/spec-kit) and [Fission-AI/OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec)). Kiro hit usage caps in preview because demand was high ([The Register, 2025-07-21](https://www.theregister.com/2025/07/21/aws_kiro_usage_cap/)). Comparison articles list 5+ SDD tools: Spec Kit, Kiro, cc-sdd, OpenSpec, Tessl, Zenflow ([issoh.co.jp](https://www.issoh.co.jp/tech/details/8732/), [InfoWorld](https://infoworld.com/article/4171332/four-cutting-edge-tools-for-spec-driven-development.html)). [Finding]
- **S2 density: medium, role-fragmented.** ChatPRD claims 100k+ PMs ([Ry Walker](https://rywalker.com/research/chatprd)). Microsoft ships Prompt Coach to Copilot users ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-in/training/modules/explore-prebuilt-microsoft-365-copilot-agents/7-examine-prompt-coach-agent)). Anthropic targets knowledge workers with Cowork plugins per job function ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-says-new-cowork-plugins-tailor-claude-specific-job-functions)). Section sells proficiency training to managers and knowledge workers ([Section](https://www.sectionai.com/ai/ai-proficiency-report)). [Finding]

- [Inference] Density by segment and channel:

| S2 semi-technical | Medium, role-specific (ChatPRD; founder framing tools) | Medium–high (Section, Prompt Coach, Cowork plugins) | High and rising (Cowork, Projects, Workspace Agents) | **Contested, with whitespace in cross-role "ambiguous problem → verifiable deliverable"** |
| S3 non-technical | Low (generic prompt improvers) | High (Multiverse, Sana, BetterUp, CoachHub, Larridin) | Medium (Prompt Coach, learning modes) | **Empty self-serve, crowded B2B** |

- [Inference] Emptiness in S3 self-serve is better read as a **weak-demand / weak-willingness-to-pay signal** than a pure gap. Individuals want outcomes, and the only S3 coaching money visible flows through employers. An NB aimed at S3 would effectively be entering the B2B AI-enablement market against funded incumbents.
- [Hypothesis] S2 is the most promising wedge, because (a) these users do have ambiguous, multi-step problems that end in verifiable artifacts, (b) role tools like ChatPRD prove willingness to pay for artifact-centric help, and (c) no SDD-equivalent "problem → workflow → verifiable deliverable" tool exists for non-code work. Risk: S2 buyers may prefer role-specific tools (counter-thesis c).

### Gaps

## Q3. What is commoditized vs. underserved? Is "adaptive coaching" + "problem-to-outcome" genuinely new or repackaging?

### Takeaway
Commoditized: prompt rewriting, Socratic and learning modes, generic AI-literacy courses, spec templates for code, workflow automation and LLM output evaluation. Underserved: (1) coaching *inside a real, ambiguous work task*, not in a course; (2) evaluating the *human's* collaboration on a specific task (vendors measure this only in aggregate or by telemetry); (3) problem-to-verifiable-deliverable structure for *non-code* work; (4) restraint, meaning coaching that knows when not to intervene. "Problem-to-outcome" is largely a repackaging of SDD and elicitation for non-code work. "Adaptive coaching" is old as a concept (tutoring systems) and is now being productized by well-funded players. NB's novelty would lie in the *combination and the measurement*, not in either concept alone.

### Cited Findings
- **Prompt improvement is commoditized and native.** Anthropic Console improver (November 2024) and OpenAI optimizer for developers ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/prompt-improver), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/291870/promptly-decrypt-ai-prompt-improver/)). Microsoft Prompt Coach for end users ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-in/training/modules/explore-prebuilt-microsoft-365-copilot-agents/7-examine-prompt-coach-agent)). Many free third-party tools ([BenchLM](https://benchlm.ai/tools/prompt-optimizer)). [Fact]
- **Socratic / guided modes are native in all three major chatbots** (2025) ([Pearson](https://pearson.com/international-schools/international-schools-blog/2026/02/what-is-gemini_s-guided-learning-and-chatgpts-study-mode-.html), [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/claudes-new-learning-modes-take-on-chatgpts-study-mode-heres-what-they-do)). [Fact]
- **AI-literacy curricula are free from the vendors**: Anthropic AI Fluency, with educator materials under CC BY-NC-SA ([Anthropic Academy](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-for-builders)). [Fact]
- **Spec/requirements structure for code is commoditized**: Spec Kit is open-source and very active; Kiro is AWS-backed ([martinfowler.com](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/sdd-3-tools.html)). Tessl moved away from spec-as-source toward an evaluated skills registry ([Tessl blog](https://tessl.io/blog/skills-are-software-and-they-need-a-lifecycle-introducing-skills-on-tessl/)). [Fact]
- **SDD's known failure modes map directly onto NB's coaching risks**: process overhead out of proportion to the task (a small bug became 16 acceptance criteria), review fatigue, a false sense of control, and no clear spec owner ([martinfowler.com, 2025-10-15](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/sdd-3-tools.html)). [Fact]
- **Collaboration evaluation exists only at population level or via telemetry**: Anthropic's Fluency Index measures 11 of 24 behaviors from chat logs and admits the unobservable ones may be the most consequential ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/research/AI-fluency-index)). Larridin sells an "AI Fluency" module to enterprises ([Larridin](https://larridin.com/press-releases/larridin-raises-30-million-in-series-a-to-prove-ais-impact-across-the-human-and-agent-workforce)). Section runs proficiency assessments ([Section](https://www.sectionai.com/ai/ai-proficiency-report)). [Fact]
- **A documented, concrete need NB could target**: polished artifacts *reduce* user discernment (less fact-checking and questioning) ([Anthropic AI Fluency Index](https://anthropic.com/research/AI-fluency-index), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-ai-fluency-index-finds-that-polished-ai-output-makes-users-less-likely-to-check-for-errors/)). This maps directly to NB's "Evaluation Gap." [Fact → Inference]
- **Research warns that adaptive coaching by LLMs over-intervenes**: MetaCLASS found 43.2% best-move accuracy and chose silence about 4% of the time when silence was correct ([arXiv 2602.02457](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.02457v1)). The Bloom LLM coach improved mindset but not the behavior outcome versus a non-LLM app ([Stanford News, Apr 2026](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/04/ai-health-coach-mindset)). [Fact]
- **"Prompting isn't enough; redesign workflows" is already an incumbent message** ([Section blog](https://www.sectionai.com/blog/being-good-at-prompting-isnt-enough)). [Fact]
- **The metacognitive-support design space (planning aids, reflective prompts, feedback) was articulated in 2024** ([Tankelevitch et al., CHI 2024](https://advait.org/files/tankelevitch_2024_GenAI_metacognition.pdf)). [Fact]

### Inferences
- [Inference] **Commoditized, so NB should not build or sell it**: prompt rewriting; Socratic toggles; AI-literacy courses and frameworks; code-spec templates; workflow execution and automation; model-output eval harnesses; personal memory.
- [Inference] **Underserved, with some evidence**:
  1. *Task-embedded discernment and verification coaching* ("before you accept this polished deliverable, check X/Y/Z against your acceptance criteria"), backed by the Fluency Index artifact finding.
  2. *Per-task evaluation of collaboration*, private to the individual rather than reported to the employer. Today this is only aggregate (Anthropic) or surveillance-style (Larridin).
  3. *Problem-to-deliverable structure for non-code work*, an "SDD for knowledge work" sized to the task. No equivalent of Spec Kit for analysts or consultants was found.
  4. *Calibrated restraint*: knowing when not to coach. Research shows LLMs are bad at this by default, which makes it a real differentiator if solved and a real risk if not.
- [Inference] **Repackaging verdict**: "Problem-to-Outcome" ≈ SDD + requirements elicitation + definition-of-done, generalized beyond code. The concept is not new; carrying it to S2 non-code work with right-sizing is the only novel part. "Adaptive coaching" ≈ intelligent tutoring and scaffolding/fading, now productized by Multiverse Atlas, Sana tutoring agents and native learning modes, so the concept is not new either. The *joint* claim (adaptive, in-task coaching that measurably improves outcome **and** collaboration, with platform-native execution) is new as a product, but it is not yet a moat. It is defensible only if NB can demonstrate measurable outcome gains, which Bloom's result shows is non-trivial.
- [Hypothesis] The most defensible wedge is narrow: "verification and acceptance-criteria coaching for S2 deliverables" (framing at the start, discernment at the end). Broad "adaptive collaboration layer" positioning collides with Anthropic's own 4D framing and with enterprise enablement vendors.

### Gaps
- No independent evidence (RCTs or field studies) that in-task AI-collaboration coaching improves *work outcomes* for knowledge workers. The Bloom result is from the health domain. Consensus-style academic search was not run in this pass, and WS4 should cover it.
- Unclear whether Larridin's "AI Fluency" module scores individuals or only aggregates; this matters for the privacy and positioning contrast.

## Q4. Competitive threats: who could most easily add this?

### Takeaway
The biggest threat is Anthropic. It owns the 4D framework, measures fluency behaviors at scale, ships Socratic learning mode and memory, and gives non-developers Cowork plus plugins, so it could ship "fluency coaching in the flow of work" as a feature. Microsoft is next: Prompt Coach plus Copilot analytics, with distribution to M365 seats. Then come enterprise enablement and measurement players (Multiverse Atlas, Larridin, Section, Sana/Workday), who could bundle in-task coaching with the measurement buyers already pay for. In S1, Spec Kit and Kiro already own the problem-to-spec flow. OpenAI is a threat with lower certainty because its builder and eval surfaces churn: Agent Builder and Evals shut down on 2026-11-30.

### Cited Findings
- Anthropic has the ingredients: the 4D framework and courses ([Anthropic Academy](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-for-builders)), a behavior-level fluency measurement method ([AI Fluency Index](https://anthropic.com/research/AI-fluency-index)), learning mode ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/claudes-new-learning-modes-take-on-chatgpts-study-mode-heres-what-they-do)), and Cowork plus job-function plugins for non-developers ([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/anthropic-brings-plugins-to-cowork/)). Anthropic says future work will assess the unobservable fluency behaviors with qualitative methods ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/research/AI-fluency-index)). [Fact]
- Microsoft: Prompt Coach is prebuilt and also shipped as a template and open-source sample ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/agent-template-prompt-coach), [PnP](https://pnp.github.io/copilot-pro-dev-samples/samples/da-PromptCoach/)). [Fact]
- OpenAI: Agent Builder is being replaced by "ChatGPT Workspace Agents", and Evals by Promptfoo, both on 2026-11-30 ([OpenAI deprecations](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations)). Study Mode is native ([Pearson](https://pearson.com/international-schools/international-schools-blog/2026/02/what-is-gemini_s-guided-learning-and-chatgpts-study-mode-.html)). [Fact]
- Enterprise enablement and measurement are well funded: Multiverse ($70M, May 2026, Atlas AI coach), Larridin ($30M, 2026-11-02, AI Fluency module), Workday/Sana ($1.1B) ([Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/128958-multiverse-raises-70m-at-2-1b-valuation-to-build-europes-ai-adoption-lay/), [Larridin](https://larridin.com/press-releases/larridin-raises-30-million-in-series-a-to-prove-ais-impact-across-the-human-and-agent-workforce), [Workday](https://newsroom.workday.com/2025-09-16-Workday-Signs-Definitive-Agreement-to-Acquire-Sana)). [Fact]
- S1: Spec Kit has very high commit cadence (local clone, 2026-11-17), and Kiro is AWS-backed with enterprise billing via IAM Identity Center ([ToolDirectory](https://tooldirectory.ai/tools/kiro)). [Fact]
- Consolidation in the enabler layer: Langfuse went to ClickHouse (2026-01-16) and Helicone to Mintlify (2026-03-03) ([ClickHouse](https://clickhouse.com/blog/clickhouse-acquires-langfuse-open-source-llm-observability), [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/two-acquisitions-deep-two-still-standing-whats-really-happening-to-open-source-llm-observability)). [Fact]

### Inferences
- [Inference] Threat ranking: ease of adding NB-like capability × distribution.

| Rank | Threat | Why they could add it | What they'd likely miss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anthropic (Claude, Cowork) | Owns the framework, measurement method, Socratic mode, memory and agent surface | Cross-platform neutrality; privacy-first individual evaluation; may stay "education" rather than in-task |
| 2 | Microsoft (M365 Copilot) | Prompt Coach plus agents plus org analytics; seat distribution in S2/S3 | Problem framing beyond prompt rewrite; individual-centric feedback |
| 3 | Multiverse / Section / Sana-Workday / BetterUp | Coaching UX plus enterprise HR budgets plus fluency taxonomies | Deep integration with native execution; real-task evaluation |
| 4 | Larridin / Workhelix | Already measure "AI Fluency" or task fit at org level | Coaching; individual trust (telemetry reads as surveillance) |
| 5 | GitHub Spec Kit / Kiro / ChatPRD | Own problem-to-spec for devs and PMs; could add "coach mode" | Non-code generality; collaboration evaluation |
| 6 | OpenAI / Google | Native modes plus memory plus Workspace Agents | Volatile product surfaces (Agent Builder and Evals sunset) |

- [Inference] Absorption horizon: the prompt-coaching and Socratic layers are already absorbed. Workflow scaffolding for non-developers is being absorbed now (Cowork plugins, Workspace Agents). Task-embedded collaboration evaluation for individuals looks like the slowest to be absorbed: vendors have so far measured it only in aggregate. [Hypothesis] I would put it at about 12–24 months before a native "fluency feedback" feature appears from Anthropic or Microsoft. That is a judgment call; no roadmap evidence was found.
- [Inference] Implication for the main question: the smallest defensible NB is not a "layer" but a sharp wedge, measurable outcome improvement on S2 ambiguous deliverables through framing plus verification coaching, used on the native platforms. It should explicitly map to the 4D vocabulary rather than invent a competing framework.

### Gaps
- No public roadmaps from Anthropic or Microsoft on individual fluency feedback features. The absorption horizon is my estimate.
- Could not verify whether Google ships an equivalent of Prompt Coach in Workspace. Not searched.
