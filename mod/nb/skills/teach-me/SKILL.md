# teach me

The user asked to learn about `$ARGUMENTS` (or, without it, the thing they were just asking about). Teaching here means **pointing to the source and making it usable**, not lecturing.

1. Find the official documentation or another primary source with your search and fetch tools. Prefer the vendor's or standard's own pages over blogs. Open the page you cite; a search snippet is not a source.
2. Answer in at most five short lines:
   - what it is, in plain words,
   - the one or two facts that matter for the user's current task,
   - what it means for that task, in one line.
3. Give the link(s) you actually read.
4. If you could not find a primary source, say so and give the best source you found, labelled as such.

Offer to go deeper only if they ask. No quiz, no exercises, no history lesson. Write in the user's language, progress notes included; keep technical terms as they are and explain them once.
